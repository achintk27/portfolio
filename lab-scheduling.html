01 / THE PROBLEM

Why build it?

Shared equipment needs a reliable reservation system. The platform gives students and researchers a single place to book equipment, while preventing conflicting reservations.

02 / THE APPROACH

How it works.

Built a React interface backed by Firebase and Vercel serverless APIs. OTP authentication controls access, while concurrency checks protect equipment and time slots from conflicting bookings. Resend supports the authentication workflow.

03 / THE OUTCOME

What it delivers.

The platform supports 200+ users. Its focus is a dependable booking flow: authenticated access, clear reservations, and safeguards at the API layer.
